# The Homegrown algorithm

A fresh, home-grown alternative to the [Autopilot](./ALGORITHM-AUTOPILOT.md).
Where the autopilot faithfully recreates the original app, this one is ours to
design from scratch — and it's the most hands-on: a single repeating **dip** you
shape live while it runs.

## Shaping it

Two controls:

- **Speed** — how fast the device runs.
- **Variability** — how the dips behave, from **off** (no dip at all — a steady
  hold at the top) through **low** and **medium** to **high** (deep, teasing
  plunges with plenty of timing variation, so no two feel quite the same). Turn it
  up for deeper, more unpredictable dips; turn it down to settle toward a steady
  stroke. Change it mid-session and it eases smoothly back up to the top before the
  new feel takes over, so it never lurches.

Even at a low Speed you still get a wide, satisfying range rather than a narrow
flutter near the top — the slower you set it, the further the dips reach down.

## Cumming

Say **cumming** for the send-off: the device eases down in a slow glide from a
moderate pace to a standstill over about fifteen seconds, drawing and holding the
vacuum to carry you through, then rests. It's a gentle, drawn-out finish — which is
simply how the author likes to come, and won't be to everyone's taste; take it as
one way to end, not the only one.

## Voice control

- **faster** / **slower** — Speed up or down.
- **off** / **low** / **medium** / **high** — set Variability.
- **cumming** — the wind-down.
- plus the shared **up** / **down** stroke words for manual vacuum control.
